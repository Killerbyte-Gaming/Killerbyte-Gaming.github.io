### Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 2026** 

This Privacy Policy explains how our application ("we," "us," or "our") handles your information when you use our services to log in and post videos to your TikTok account. 

### 1. Information We Access and Collect

When you connect your TikTok account to our application, we access specific information via the official TikTok API to enable posting features: 

* **TikTok Account Authentication:** We use secure login tokens (OAuth) to authenticate your identity. We do not see or store your TikTok password.
* **Profile Information:** We may access basic public profile details, such as your TikTok username and profile picture, to display your active session within the app.
* **Media Content:** We temporarily process the videos, captions, and related metadata you upload to our application for the sole purpose of publishing them to your TikTok account.

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the accessed data strictly to provide the app's core functionality: 

* To log you into our application securely.
* To transmit and publish your selected videos directly to your TikTok account at your direction.
* To troubleshoot technical issues related to video uploading.

### 3. Data Retention and Storage

* **Authentication Tokens:** Access tokens are stored securely on your device or our encrypted servers to keep you logged in.
* **Video Files:** Once a video is successfully transmitted and posted to TikTok, it is deleted from our temporary processing servers. We do not permanently store or archive your video content.

### 4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or media files with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is shared exclusively with **TikTok** to execute your request to post content. Your use of TikTok through this integration is subject to the [TikTok Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/row/privacy-policy/en). 

### 5. Revoking Access and Disconnecting

You can disconnect our application from your TikTok account at any time. To completely revoke our app's access, go to your TikTok settings under **Security and Login > Manage App Permissions** and remove our app.
